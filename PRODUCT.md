# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 14 App Router (existing). Self-host fonts; no external font CDN at runtime.

## Users

Primary user: working families and senior households in Okkiyam Thoraipakkam, OMR, and greater Chennai who need to hire vetted domestic staff (cooks, maids, nannies, elderly/patient caregivers, drivers). They are typically time-poor, often referred by word-of-mouth, and reach out on WhatsApp because it is the lowest-friction channel for a quick, personal conversation. Secondary user: families outside India arranging care for parents living in Chennai.

## Product Purpose

Match Chennai households with vetted, identity-checked domestic staff and stay personally reachable for the lifetime of the placement. Success means a family places a helper within days of first contact and stays in touch with the agency for as long as the placement lasts.

## Positioning

A neighbourhood manpower agency on the OMR tech corridor, run directly by Thomas R. No IVR, no call-centre routing — every enquiry reaches a person who has met the staff being recommended. That direct relationship is the offer competitors with larger footprints cannot truthfully copy.

## Operating Context

Enquiries come in via WhatsApp (primary), phone calls, and the website contact form. Office is at Nehru Nagar, Okkiyam Thoraipakkam (landmark: Back Side Cognizant), within practical reach of families on the OMR corridor. Service area covers OMR, Perungudi, Sholinganallur, Velachery, Adyar, Semmancheri, Navalur, Tambaram, Medavakkam, and adjacent zones.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Colours Life Manpower Agency. Visual identity restyle for dark surfaces is allowed; the name and the founder's name stay.
- Voice: direct, calm, family-first. No marketing hype, no automated chat personas.
- Verification: Aadhaar check, residential reference check, role/task clarity before commencement.
- Reachability: WhatsApp and phone are first-class; the website exists to convert to a WhatsApp conversation.

## Product Principles

1. WhatsApp is the conversion event. Every page section exists to get a family to a WhatsApp message.
2. Specificity earns trust. Names, addresses, languages spoken, and case-study localities stay concrete.
3. Founder-direct. The site must always make clear that the person answering is Thomas R, not a bot.
4. Service breadth over service novelty. Cover the eight established services well before adding any new category.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific accessibility standard has been stated. Treat the surface as a public commercial website: WCAG AA contrast on text, keyboard reachable controls, descriptive alt text on every photograph.
